Version 1.0 is the first release of Speedwell.

Pour one cocktail from bottles already on your rail. Add bottles on Bar as Backbar, Crack them onto the rail, Recork to lift them, and Pin a recipe you will make again. Discover lists Makeable recipes versus Missing a seated bottle. Settings can Replay walkthrough, Reset the well, or Contact Speedwell.

The recipe book includes Negroni, Martini, Manhattan, Old Fashioned, Boulevardier, Daiquiri, Margarita, Paloma, French 75, Whiskey Sour, Espresso Martini, and Gin Rickey.
